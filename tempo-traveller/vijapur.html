Key Sightseeing & Heritage Spots in Vijayapura

Our driver will take your group to all prime monuments with full waiting time included.

🏛️

Gol Gumbaz

Iconic 17th-century mausoleum with one of the world's largest unsupported masonry domes and the Whispering Gallery.

🕌

Ibrahim Rauza

Known as the "Taj Mahal of the Deccan", featuring exquisite minarets, intricate stone carvings, and tranquil gardens.

🏛️

Bara Kaman

Dramatic unfinished mausoleum of Ali Adil Shah II showcasing 12 graceful concentric stone arches.

🛡️

Malik-e-Maidan & Fort

Legendary 55-tonne medieval bronze cannon and historic citadel ramparts reflecting Adil Shahi military heritage.

🕉️

Shivagiri Statue

Imposing 85-foot towering statue of Lord Shiva, a serene spiritual attraction on the outskirts of Vijayapura.

🕍

Jama Masjid & Gagan Mahal

Ancient royal congregation mosque with graceful arches and the royal audience hall overlooking palace grounds.

🗺️

Badami & Hampi Extension

Multi-day circuits to Badami Cave Temples, Pattadakal, Aihole, and UNESCO World Heritage site Hampi.

💍

Weddings & Family Events

Comfortable group transit between wedding halls, hotels, and Solapur-Vijayapura venues.
