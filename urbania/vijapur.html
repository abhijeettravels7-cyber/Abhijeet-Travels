Key Sightseeing Spots in Vijayapura

Our driver will take your group to all prime monuments with full waiting time included.

Gol Gumbaz

The iconic 17th-century mausoleum with one of the world's largest unsupported masonry domes and the mesmerizing Whispering Gallery.

Ibrahim Rauza

Known as the "Taj Mahal of the Deccan", this magnificent architectural complex features exquisite minarets, intricate stone carvings, and tranquil gardens.

Bara Kaman

The dramatic unfinished mausoleum of Ali Adil Shah II, showcasing 12 graceful concentric stone arches reaching toward the sky.

Malik-e-Maidan & Fort

The legendary 55-tonne medieval bronze cannon and historic citadel ramparts reflecting the grand military heritage of the Adil Shahi Sultanate.

Shivagiri Statue

An imposing 85-foot towering statue of Lord Shiva, a serene and popular spiritual stop on the outskirts of Vijayapura city.

Jama Masjid & Gagan Mahal

Ancient royal congregation mosque with graceful arches and the royal audience hall overlooking medieval palace gardens.
