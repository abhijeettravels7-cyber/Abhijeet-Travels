Prime Kolhapur Sightseeing & Pilgrim Attractions

Our chauffeur will guide your group across all holy sites and historical landmarks with driver waiting included.

Shri Mahalakshmi (Ambabai) Temple

The sacred 7th-century Chalukyan Shakti Peetha, renowned for the magical Kirnotsav and divine presence of Goddess Mahalakshmi.

Panhala Fort

The legendary hill fortress where Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj spent significant days, offering breathtaking views, Sajja Kothi, and ancient granaries.

Jyotiba Temple (Wadi Ratnagiri)

Perched at 3,124 feet on Wadi Ratnagiri hills, celebrated for vibrant pink Gulal festivities and grand spiritual blessings.

Rankala Lake & Chowpatty

Historic scenic lake flanked by the Shalini Palace and Sandhya Math, famous for tranquil evening strolls and authentic Kolhapuri bhel.

New Palace & Chhatrapati Shahu Museum

Stunning Indo-Saracenic palace museum housing rare royal artifacts, weaponry, royal costumes, and heritage memorabilia of Kolhapur's royalty.

Kaneri Math (Siddhagiri Museum)

Unique open-air rural museum showcasing life-sized clay sculptures that vividly illustrate self-sufficient ancient Indian village culture.
